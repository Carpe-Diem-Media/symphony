### Retained constraints and blockers

E is native root evidence and control, I is the boundary evidence index, R is the resource ledger, and A is the existing attempt ledger. The proposed compatibility path keeps these owners and the original C01 attempt identity.

- **One authority and durable history.** The schema-1 C01 pilot row owns consumption and result. The compatibility design must preserve existing exact identity and replay refusal and avoid implicit migration. Actual legacy release can replace the whole fence map. Protect the complete writer set and exclude historical binaries before writable open. The proposed protected launcher, independent witness and rollback exclusion still need an exact contract and qualification, including the native E writer's participation without a dependency cycle.
- **Resource ownership and settlement.** Preserve P01 rows. The separate C01 resource store and disjoint metadata, index and resource keys are proposed. It needs closed row and journal formats, exact membership, revision and generation guards, and old/new/mixed recovery proof. Record allocation and destruction intent before effects. Bind the absence snapshot to synchronized `DESTROYING` revisions. Observe complete absence and references before verified R mutation. Retain original E/I/R/A authentication, ordering and separate fresh reads at completion recording and settlement. C01 A may record only an exact resource-settlement commitment after E preparation. It conveys no legacy fence release, rearm or new authority.
- **Containment under uncertainty.** An exact authorized owner may pursue safe identity-bound containment and disposal while custody remains retained. Failed journal closure does not authorize destruction and does not bar otherwise authorized safe actions. It bars positive settlement. Missing death proof blocks destruction wherever safe removal depends on proven writer death. Missing or conflicting observations lead to quarantine or unresolved retention. C04 and ambiguous external effects preserve their original outcomes.
- **Protocol closure.** Missing pieces include the literal compatibility grammar for each affected native envelope, record and reference, closed manifest and evidence unions, field-level acyclic digest dependencies, index and root/recovery bindings, and positive and negative byte vectors. No implementation may infer these from R1–R3 prose. Further compatibility amendment revisions remain on hold.
- **Stability.** The original [partition 8 failure](https://github.com/Carpe-Diem-Media/symphony/actions/runs/45049761956/job/137292081921) reported two `authority_fence_terminal` fixture failures and one `storage_unavailable` fixture failure. The [same-head retry](https://github.com/Carpe-Diem-Media/symphony/actions/runs/45049761956/job/137294203709) passed 264 tests. Cause remains unresolved. Later qualification should exercise admission and reconciliation orderings and inspect actual durable consumption and fence outcomes.
