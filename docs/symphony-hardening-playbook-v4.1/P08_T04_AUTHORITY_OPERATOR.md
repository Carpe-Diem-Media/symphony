Execution-grant signing belongs to the owner role. The reviewer remains independent of the producer operator, preparer and build guest. Preserve original public-key confirmations, decoded SSH-wire fingerprints, custody records, role/namespace scope, validity dates and the owner's enrollment decision. A matching hash, locally signed record or `ACCEPTED` field cannot supply that identity.

Freeze the verifier source V and public vector definitions before constructing P. Independently review the complete OpenSSH, interpreter, source/import, standard-library, native-extension, loader/library and configuration inventory. The verifier profile P binds those inputs, the exact allowed-signers bytes, operator appointment and status/clock/custody/register procedures. Original E accepts exact canonical SHA-256(P) outside P. P must not contain E's hash, the later native source C1, a manifest or a result hash. Qualification results follow P and receive separate acceptance.

The appointed independent operator must authenticate E against the already known human owner's approved channel before installing P. The verifier cannot authenticate that human merely by reading E. Trusted installation must prevent the preparer and guest from changing V, P/E, allowed-signers, inputs or the status/register. Mode bits on a user-owned writable directory do not establish this separation. Missing host, principal, custody or runtime evidence blocks operational acceptance. No installer, privilege or replacement service is included here.

## Verification input and current observations

The library API receives an independently established trusted context from the operator. It is never selected by a manifest, a CLI trust flag or an environment Boolean. The context contains the installed P, exact authenticated profile bytes, fixed signature/bundle custody, source observations and independent current status. The implementation docstrings define the required fields. Callers must not populate that context from a guest's assertions. Unit tests explicitly supply synthetic facts; they do not attest human identity or real custody.

Check exact raw canonical bytes before interpreting records. Unknown/missing/duplicate fields, unsupported values, escapes, whitespace, final newline, ambiguous integers and size excess refuse. Every reference must resolve to the exact earlier bytes. Verify S/R/O/G and B with the fixed principal, role file, namespace and pinned SSHSIG executable. M is an input description authenticated transitively by the separately signed R/O/G; it is not an owner execution signature.

Record and signature files use bounded no-follow, nonblocking regular-file reads. Size and stable file identity are checked before and after reading. The operator must still provide immutable custody through verification and launch; these reads cannot establish human approval or prevent an actor with write access from replacing an input later.

R's INPUT_REVIEW_ONLY observation has a review ID and cannot prove launch freshness. G's ACTION_PREFLIGHT_ONLY report has the exact preallocated grant/run/nonce/command/source/input bindings and independent monotonic session. The 60-second completion-to-launch ceiling includes signing and queue delays. Input validity is at most 86,400 seconds, action lease at most 900 seconds, UTC uncertainty at most 30 seconds. The entire uncertainty interval must remain valid. An active lease never extends observation freshness.

Resolve each executable through its exact approved path, inventory entry, regular endpoint and declared leaf-link chain. The actual exec path and argv[0] must match. Same-content files at other paths, directory aliases, undeclared links and PATH lookup refuse. Independently controlled sealed custody must prevent replacement after observation. A single digest cannot prove that custody.

## One-use and failure procedure

A separately appointed operator establishes the register directory and current epoch under protected custody. The gate never initializes a missing operational register. It locks the exact inode, checks canonical existing history and the epoch, then creates no-replace grant/run/nonce markers and synchronizes files and directory before launch. A partial transaction burns every identity referenced by its surviving marker. Corrupt or uncertain history refuses. Register exhaustion refuses rather than removing old evidence.

The same gate process performs the final status, clock, path and custody check after consumption. No later independent shell launch may reuse a read-only verifier's success report. Failed exec, late signing, stale observation or uncertain synchronization never permits replay. Retain all original markers and terminal events. A new attempt needs a new G/run/nonce and fresh preflight. No record is edited to renew a lease or replace a decision.

Monitor the finite command under the accepted external status and termination procedure. Observed revocation, lease expiry, integrity failure, unknown status, incomplete descendant disposition or unexpected effects stop the attempt under that already granted procedure. The source tests cover finite sentinel processes. They do not qualify arbitrary process containment, a filesystem's power-loss behavior or knowledge of an unseen remote revocation.

Output collection continues status and lease checks and retains at most 65,536 bytes per stream. A failed action exposes its bounded output, truncation status and exit metadata on the exception's `result` attribute for the appointed caller to retain under the approved evidence profile. A `COMPLETED` result means the exact finite process exited successfully. It grants no qualification acceptance and produces no signed compilation-result B.

After compilation, successful exit alone grants no test execution. Authenticate the successful producer history and sealed output inventory/custody in B. Construct a new M_test, obtain fresh independent R_test and explicit O_test, then issue a separate G_test for an already approved exact generated executable. B and its output hashes cannot replace that chain. Failed or ambiguous builds are terminally ineligible. No output may be invoked even with `--list` before its separate approval.
